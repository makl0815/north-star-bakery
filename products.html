Our Breads, Pastries, and Cakes

Everything is baked in small batches, so favorites can sell out by afternoon. Prices are ranges and depend on size. There is no online checkout: send a pre-order request and we will confirm your pickup.

Breads

Price range: $6 to $12 per loaf

  • Country sourdough: open crumb and a crisp, blistered crust
  • Seeded whole grain: hearty and good for sandwiches
  • Baguettes: baked in the morning and again at noon

Pastries

Price range: $3 to $7 each

  • Butter croissants: laminated by hand over two days
  • Seasonal fruit danishes: made with fruit from nearby farms
  • Cookies: chocolate chunk, brown sugar, and oatmeal

Cakes

Price range: $28 to $65 for a whole cake, $5 to $7 per slice

  • Lemon poppy seed cake: light, with a tangy glaze
  • Chocolate layer cake: dark chocolate sponge and ganache
  • Custom celebration cakes: please order at least 48 hours ahead
