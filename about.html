About North Star Bakery

Our story

North Star Bakery started as a small neighborhood shop with one oven and a handful of regulars. The idea was simple: bake good bread every day and treat everyone who walks in like a neighbor.

Today we still bake in small batches, and most mornings the first loaves are out of the oven before the street wakes up.

Where our ingredients come from

We buy from people we know whenever we can. Our flour comes from regional mills, our butter and eggs from nearby farms, and our fruit from whatever is in season. Shorter distances mean fresher ingredients, and we like knowing who grew what goes into our dough.
